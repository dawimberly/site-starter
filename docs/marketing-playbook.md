# Marketing playbook

## Start with the business
Document the audience, geography, services, capacity, offer, desired lead and a measurable objective. Verify claims and customer permissions. A consulting practice may need qualified conversations and professional credibility; a local contractor may prioritize nearby calls and project inquiries. Select channels to fit the business rather than enabling everything.

## Foundation
Make the offer and next action clear. Create useful service pages only where they answer distinct customer needs. Set up Search Console and, for eligible businesses, a client-owned Google Business Profile. Use real service areas and approved photos; avoid invented claims, reviews or locations.

## Measurement
Define a measurement plan before buying traffic: source, landing page, contact action, actual qualified lead and eventual outcome. Track events only after consent and privacy review where applicable. A click is not proof of a call or a qualified lead. Confirm delivery and test conversions end to end before relying on reports. Avoid collecting personal information in analytics.

Use consistent lowercase UTM values, for example `utm_source=google&utm_medium=cpc&utm_campaign=kitchen_san_antonio_2026q4&utm_content=estimate_a`; document a naming table for each client and never include personal details. Define events separately: `contact_click` is a tap, `lead_accepted` is a server-confirmed provider acceptance, and `qualified_lead` is a human-verified opportunity. Provider acceptance is not proof of inbox delivery. Specify which event is an advertising conversion and validate it before enabling bidding.

## Ads and social
With a client-approved budget and goal, build a small, relevant campaign with landing pages aligned to each offer. Review search terms, targeting, exclusions, creative claims and costs regularly. Start paid traffic only after the contact and measurement flows are verified. For social, select appropriate channels and a sustainable cadence; obtain approval and media rights before publishing.

## Improve
Record spend, inquiries, qualified leads, booked work and revenue where available. Review by period and source, noting missing data. Run one clear change at a time when practical. Do not promise outcomes based on a single click or short test.
